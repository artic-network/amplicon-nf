Summaries of the per-sample align_trim QC reports (<SAMPLE>.summary.tsv, .amplicons.tsv, .primer_pairs.tsv, .split_reads.tsv, .overlaps.tsv). A fragment is a single read for Nanopore and a read pair for Illumina.

How primers are identified. Primers are assigned to a fragment by position, not sequence: each aligned end (leftmost aligned start, rightmost aligned end, soft-clipped bases excluded) is given the nearest primer pointing inwards from an amplicon whose span, extended by {{ aligntrim.primer_threshold | safe }}, covers it. Checks of whether an end is “at” a primer compare its aligned coordinate with the primer's 5′ end, within {{ aligntrim.primer_threshold | safe }}. The only sequence-based check is the Also observed primer pair columns, which search the read ends for primer sequence. Each definition in the keys is tagged with what it is judged on.

A join is where a read's alignment stops and continues at another position, strand or reference, taken from the aligner's supplementary alignments (SA tag). Every fragment that passes the alignment checks (Read Fates unmapped to no_alignment) is checked, whatever its later fate; breakpoints are judged by position against primer 5′ ends, not by sequence. Joins are grouped by their two breakpoints (in 10 bp windows) and orientation; a join seen in both mates of a pair counts once. The first number is fragment-joins (fragments showing that kind of join, summed over joins), the number in brackets is how many distinct joins were seen. Per-join detail, including breakpoint positions and orientation, is in <SAMPLE>.split_reads.tsv.

{{ render_key(aligntrim.split_reads.key) }}

Overlaps between neighbouring amplicons (n/n+1): the largest difference in base frequency between the two amplicons' kept, correctly paired fragments, where their inserts overlap. Blank cells were not compared. Fragments removed as ambiguous inside overlaps are counted in Read Fates (ambiguous_amplicon).
